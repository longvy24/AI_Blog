8 Prompts So Your App
Doesn’t Look Like Every AI App

The purple gradient, the glassy cards, the same font every other AI app uses. Eight prompts that get you a design with an actual point of view.

R
Ryu Vy
AIToolsDaily24 · By a human, for humans

You can spot an AI-built app from across the room: the purple gradient, the glassy cards, the same font every other AI app uses. Left to its defaults, the model builds the same page every single time.

Getting something that doesn’t look generated is mostly about banning those defaults and handing it a real reference to work from. Eight prompts that get you a design with an actual point of view.

The Eight

Every Move, Copy-Paste Ready

01 / 08Ban
Kill the Defaults
"No purple gradient, no glass cards, no Inter. Pick something else and tell me why."
Left alone it reaches for the same three choices every single time.
02 / 08Reference
Copy a Real Feeling
"Design this like a 1970s transit sign, not like a SaaS landing page."
A reference outside software is the fastest way out of the template.
03 / 08Color
One Accent, That's It
"One accent color on a neutral base. Use it for exactly one job."
Three accent colors is how a page stops having a point of focus.
04 / 08Type
Two Fonts, Big Gap
"One display face, one body face. Make the size gap obvious, not subtle."
Timid type scales are why everything reads as ‘generic startup’.
05 / 08Space
Give It Room
"Double every margin. Then delete a third of the elements on the page."
Crowding is the default because the model has nothing to lose by adding.
06 / 08Voice
Write Like a Person
"Rewrite every label. No 'seamlessly', no 'empower', no 'unlock'."
The copy gives it away faster than the layout does.
07 / 08Edges
Empty and Broken
"Design the empty state and the error state, not just the happy path."
Real products are mostly edge cases. Demos are mostly the happy path.
08 / 08Squint
The Blur Test
"Describe this page blurred. Is the hierarchy still readable?"
If nothing survives the blur, nothing was actually emphasized.
