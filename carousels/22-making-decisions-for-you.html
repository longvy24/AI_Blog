9 Prompts to Stop
AI Making the Decision for You

It's very good at sounding certain. The expensive part was never the wrong pick — it's that I stopped comparing the moment it picked one.

R
Ryu Vy
AIToolsDaily24 · By a human, for humans

It's very good at sounding certain, and I've taken its first recommendation more times than I'd like to admit. The expensive part was never the occasional wrong answer.

It was that I stopped comparing the moment it picked one. These nine prompts force the alternatives back onto the table so the call stays yours. Copy-paste.

The Nine

Every Prompt, Copy-Paste Ready

01 / 09Options
Give Me the Trade-Off
"Don't recommend one. List the three options and what each one costs me."
The moment it recommends, you stop comparing. Make it lay out the field instead.
02 / 09Evidence
Show Your Working
"What exactly are you basing that on, and what would change it?"
A recommendation you can't trace is a coin flip in a confident voice.
03 / 09Reversal
Argue the Other Side
"Now make the strongest case against what you just told me to do."
If the opposite case is weak you've confirmed it. If it's strong you just saved yourself.
04 / 09Assumptions
Name What You Assumed
"List every assumption you made about my situation that I never told you."
It fills the gaps silently. This is the only way to see what it filled them with.
05 / 09Stakes
Tell Me What Breaks
"If this is wrong: what breaks, when do I find out, and can I undo it?"
Cheap and reversible needs no thought. Expensive and permanent needs yours.
06 / 09Defaults
Say What Most People Do
"What's the boring default here, and what would have to be true to beat it?"
The default is usually right. Make the clever answer earn its place.
07 / 09Confidence
Rate Your Certainty
"Score your confidence 1 to 10, and say what's pulling the number down."
It will not volunteer doubt. Asked directly, it is surprisingly honest.
08 / 09Framing
Ask What I Left Out
"What's the question I should be asking that I haven't asked?"
Most bad decisions are right answers to the wrong question.
09 / 09Ownership
Make Me Say It
"Don't answer yet. Ask me the three questions you need answered first."
If you can't answer its three questions, you weren't ready to decide anyway.
