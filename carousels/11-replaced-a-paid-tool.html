8 Prompts That Replaced
a Tool I Paid For

One AI chat quietly ate half my app subscriptions. Here's the exact prompt that replaced each one.

R
Ryu Vy
AIToolsDaily24 · By a human, for humans

I didn't plan to cancel anything. But one by one, the little single-purpose apps stopped earning their spot — because one AI chat, with the right prompt, quietly did the same job. Grammar checker, summarizer, translator, colour picker: gone, replaced by eight prompts I keep in a note.

Here they are, exactly as I use them. None of this needs a special tool — just the chat you already have open and the right ask.

The Eight

Every Prompt, Copy-Paste Ready

01 / 08Grammar
Fix My Writing
"Proofread this. Fix grammar and flow, keep my voice, list each change: [text]"
Replaces the grammar checker. The change-list is the upgrade — you see what it fixed and why, so you learn instead of blindly accepting.
02 / 08Summarize
TL;DR Anything
"TL;DR this in 3 bullets, then one line on whether it's worth my time: [paste]"
Replaces the read-later summarizer. The verdict line saves the click — it tells you whether to open the thing at all.
03 / 08Design
Name the Palette
"Give me a 5-colour palette for [mood] with hex codes and where to use each."
Replaces the colour-picker app. Hex plus a usage note means you paste it straight into your design instead of eyeballing swatches.
04 / 08Naming
Brainstorm Names
"25 name ideas for [thing]. Short, no hyphens, and flag any obviously taken."
Replaces the name generator. The constraints kill the junk — no three-word hyphenated messes, no names of famous brands.
05 / 08Rewrite
Match a Tone
"Rewrite this in the tone of [example]. Same facts, their voice: [text]"
Replaces the paraphrase tool. It mirrors a real writing sample instead of a preset “formal / casual” slider — much closer to what you actually want.
06 / 08Data
Clean This Up
"Turn this messy list into a tidy markdown table. Fix obvious typos: [paste]"
Replaces the spreadsheet cleanup. It infers the columns you'd have built by hand and catches the typos you'd have missed.
07 / 08Translate
Localize, Don't Translate
"Translate to [language], but make it sound native — not literal. Flag the untranslatable."
Replaces the translator. “Native, not literal” is the difference that matters — and flagging what won't carry over saves you an embarrassing mistranslation.
08 / 08Plan
Turn It Into Steps
"Break [goal] into a checklist I can start today. Order by what unblocks the most."
Replaces the project template. Ordering by leverage — what unblocks the most next — is the part generic templates always skip.
